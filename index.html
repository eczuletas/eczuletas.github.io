About me

My background and research interests.

I am an energy scientist with experience spanning redox flow batteries, sodium-based energy storage, redox-active molecules, and sustainable materials.

My work brings together synthesis, spectroscopy, and electrochemical testing. I enjoy moving between fundamental questions about molecules and interfaces and the practical work needed to make energy systems perform reliably

Research areas

Turning chemistry into durable energy storage.

01

Molecular synthesis

Synthesis and characterization of redox-active molecules and energy-storage materials using NMR, Raman, UV–Vis, and EPR spectroscopy.

02

Redox flow batteries

Design and electrochemical evaluation of catholytes, interfaces, and cell architectures for long-duration energy storage.

03

Sodium-based systems

Sodium-metal hybrid batteries, solid-electrolyte interfaces, and sodium thiophosphate and polysulfide chemistry.

04

Electrochemical diagnostics

Impedance spectroscopy, equivalent-circuit modeling, voltammetry, cycling, and flow-cell testing.

Teaching experience

Teaching engineering and experimental methods.

My teaching philosophy is based on my belief that engineering students learn best when they can connect fundamental concepts with experiments and real-world applications. I aim to create a supportive learning environment in which students feel comfortable asking questions, developing their analytical skills, and applying what they learn to solve practical problems.

2010–2012

Lecturer and Teaching Assistant

Universidad de Antioquia, Faculty of Engineering

Taught Statistics and Design of Experiments, assisted with the Heat Transfer Laboratory, and provided instruction and statistical consulting for food design and formulation.
